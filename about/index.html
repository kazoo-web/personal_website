Hi, I'm Katherine.

I was raised by families of artists and scientists, which probably explains why I get as excited about a well-crafted sentence as I do about a well-structured decision-making process or a dashboard that provides a novel business insight.

My work lives at the intersection of organizational design, people strategy, and operations — with a persistent interest in the underlying question:

How do we build organizations that actually work effectively toward their missions? Not in theory, but under pressure, with real constraints, across people who don't always agree.

That question has pulled me across a few different contexts.

Most recently
Electrified Thermal Solutions
VP of Operations at a climate tech startup — building operational infrastructure through a critical growth phase.
Before that · 5 years
Paradigm
Led data and consulting teams at a strategy consulting firm, building a core product at the intersection of organizational research and practice.
🎓

My academic grounding is in organizational sociology and quantitative methods. I completed a Master's in Business Science at UC Berkeley's Haas School of Management before leaving the PhD program to do the work directly — which has never felt like the wrong call.

I'm drawn to mission-driven organizations navigating real complexity: the kind where strategy, people, and operations have to all move together. The problems I find most interesting sit at the intersection of how institutions are designed, how decisions get made, and how people do their best work inside imperfect systems.

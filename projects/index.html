Baby Sex & Date Betting Pool App

Complete

This app is a fun way for expectant parents to get their friends and family in on the excitement. Loved ones can purchase tickets to guess the baby's sex and arrival date and time — and the ticket proceeds go straight to the parents as a little gift to help them after baby arrives.

SignalHire Job Criteria Tool

In Progress

This is a step-by-step tool that helps you build a smarter hiring process. Paste in a job description and it guides you through four stages: identifying what you're actually looking for in a candidate, deciding how to evaluate those qualities, generating interview questions and assessments tailored to the role, and designing how your interviews should be structured. At the end, you download a ready-to-use hiring guide your whole team can follow.
